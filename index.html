About Kowsalya

Therapy that starts with being heard.

I am Kowsalya — a consultant counselling psychologist and a couple & family therapist in solo practice. Across four years and more than ten thousand conversations, my work focuses on practical, CBT-informed steps.

We name what is happening, understand why it keeps happening, and build small, repeatable ways out of it — whether you are carrying it alone or bringing it in as a couple or a family.
